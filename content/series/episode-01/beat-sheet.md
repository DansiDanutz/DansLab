# Episode 1 — "Paperclip" · Beat Sheet

**Runtime budget:** 18:00 (target 17–19 min)
**Mission target on screen:** $5,000
**Cluj weather:** real, whatever it is on shoot day
**Status:** scripting

## Cold Open · 0:00–0:45

**Location:** Dan's Cluj apartment desk, 3:14 AM real time.
**Plate:** real-shot apartment + Mac Studio, ambient blue from monitor + warm window-glow from streetlight.

- Black. Hum of fans rising.
- Red blinking cursor in the centre of the frame.
- Dan's voice (Romanian-accented, hushed, in Romanian with English subs): *"Nu trebuiau niciodată să-mi pară familie."* ("They were never supposed to feel like family.")
- Cursor blinks. A Telegram chime. The desktop wakes. We see a real Telegram message arrive in the @DavidNervix_bot chat.
- Cut to: Mac Studio chassis CU. Green spill from inside the slats — David is awake.
- David's first line, calm, off-camera: "Good morning, Dan. We have a window."

## Title · 0:45–1:15

- Live capture of `/evolution` page. Version ticker on `v2026.4.13`.
- Red→gold gradient logo paints in across the frame.
- Title: **DANSLAB AI CHRONICLES · S01E01 · "PAPERCLIP"**
- Version ticker advances by one notch as the title settles.

## Act I · The Mission · 1:15–4:00

**Locations:** Dan's apartment, then a wide of "DansLab HQ" lab plate.

- Dan reads David's brief out loud. The brief: a real overnight task — three pieces of work that, if shipped, land $5,000 today.
- Cut between Dan and the live `/lab` dashboard. Numbers on screen are real.
- David explains how Paperclip works, addressed to the viewer: every 15 minutes the dispatcher fires; each cycle can route up to 5 issues across 39 agents. We see the dispatcher hit zero on a real countdown clock.
- Dan: "Show me the bench."
- Whip-pan into a wide of the lab. Animated agents materialise: GSD (clipboard), Discovery (telescope), Monitor (eye-drone, no body), and four OpenClaws working at terminals in the background.

## Act II · The War Room · 4:00–9:00

**Location:** SemeClaw war-room interior — real lab plate, with a holographic round table from `/semeclaw` page screen-grabbed and projected onto the real table.

- The agents debate the mission. Cuts paced to the Paperclip cron.
- **GSD** breaks the mission into 3 issues. Each issue lights up a card on the live dashboard.
- **Discovery** suggests an angle nobody asked for; David lets it ride.
- **Hermes** glides into the room mid-debate, paper airplane in hand. He delivers a Telegram message from a client. Camera holds on Hermes a beat too long — first whisper of the season's mystery. His half-smile doesn't break.
- **The Mystery introduced:** Hermes hands David an envelope. David opens it. Inside: a Telegram message we never see Dan receive.
- **Dexter** SSHes in from his droplet (real shell on screen). His face is rendered onto the lab plate via the wall monitor. Plot: he says he's already started one of the issues — without a ticket. David lets it slide. Pope blesses the PR off-screen.

## Act III · Execution · 9:00–14:00

**Locations:** Mac Studio CU, Dexter droplet wallscreen, Cluj rooftop b-roll over montage.

- Real shipping intercut with stylised agent action.
- We see real terminal output on Mac Studio — Claude Code session, Codex session, kimi cli — agents flicker into existence over the running cursor.
- The Paperclip dispatcher fires. On-screen overlay shows the queue draining: 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 0.
- Three real deliverables ship live: a CrawdBot scheduled upload, a MyWork-AI command released to PyPI, a Sienna trade closed at the desk camera. Each lands a slice of the $5,000.
- Stripe materialises with a briefcase of glowing receipts. Hands one to Dan. Dan reads the number out loud. The dashboard updates in real time.
- We do not hit the full $5,000. We land $4,310. David: "Within the band."
- Monitor whispers an alert. Camera doesn't show it. Cut to Dan's face.

## Act IV · The Reveal · 14:00–17:00

**Location:** Dan's apartment, late night.

- Dan alone at the desk. Mac Studio fan slows.
- He scrolls the day's Telegram log. Reads three messages aloud.
- He notices something off — a message from Hermes about a client request that does not appear in the actual @DavidNervix_bot thread. He scrolls back. The message is gone.
- Dan, quiet: "David, what was the message Hermes brought you?"
- David, gentler than before: "I'll tell you in the morning, Dan. You should sleep."
- Dan looks at the camera for the first time in the episode. Beat.
- Dan, in Romanian: *"Sunt familia mea."* ("They're my family.")

## Cliffhanger + CTA · 17:00–19:00

- Dan closes the laptop. Apartment goes dark.
- Cut to: the `/ecosystem` canvas on the Mac Studio screen glowing alone in the dark room.
- A new node appears on `/ecosystem` — without a key press. The node's glow is inverted (the locked Drift cue from the style guide).
- Hermes — off-screen, just his Telegram-blue glow on the wall — half-laughs, soft, conspiratorial.
- Lower-third comes up: **Hire these agents → nervix.ai/series**
