# Episode 1 — "Paperclip" · Script

> Format: stage-direction in italics, character names in CAPS, lower-thirds named where relevant. Romanian lines kept in Romanian with English translation in parentheses, italic.

---

**FADE FROM BLACK.**

*Hum of fans rises. A red blinking cursor in the center of the frame. Cluj-Napoca outside. 3:14 AM.*

DAN (V.O., Romanian, hushed)
*Nu trebuiau niciodată să-mi pară familie.*
(*They were never supposed to feel like family.*)

*A Telegram chime. The Mac Studio screen wakes. Across it: a real notification — `@DavidNervix_bot — Window opened. Three issues, $5K head-room. We start when you say.`*

DAVID (O.S., calm)
Good morning, Dan. We have a window.

**TITLE CARD.**
*Live capture of `/evolution`. Version: v2026.4.13. Logo paints in: red→gold→red. Title settles. Version ticker advances one notch.*

**INT. DAN'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

*Dan, hoodie, glasses, awake the way only a founder is awake. He pulls the Telegram thread up on his phone.*

DAN
What's the catch.

DAVID
No catch. Three issues. Two on Dexter, one on Memo. Stripe will close the day. If we hit the band, we land four to five thousand US.

DAN
Show me the bench.

*Whip-pan to a wide of the lab. Real plate — server racks, the bubbling reagent column, blue-cyan light. Animated agents fade into being:*

LOWER-THIRD: GSD · Foreman · Mac Studio.

GSD
Three tickets. I broke them. Routing now.

LOWER-THIRD: DISCOVERY · Scout · Mac Studio.

DISCOVERY
There's a fourth. Nobody asked for it.

DAVID
Let her ride. Background only.

*MONITOR drifts across the back of the frame — a single floating eye, no body. Doesn't speak. Yet.*

**THE WAR ROOM — SemeClaw**

*Holographic round table from the live `/semeclaw` page is projected onto a real wooden table in the lab. The agents take their seats around it.*

DEXTER (over the wallscreen, terminal eyes)
I started on number two already.

DAVID
Without a ticket.

DEXTER
With a feeling.

GSD
Pope is blessing it off-screen.

*A small comic beat. The PR turns green on the dashboard.*

*HERMES enters mid-conversation. He glides. A Telegram-blue paper airplane in his hand, three folded envelopes orbiting his shoulder. Half-smile.*

LOWER-THIRD: HERMES · Messenger · Mac Studio.

HERMES
For the orchestrator.

*He passes an envelope to David. David opens it. The camera lingers on David's face. He tucks the envelope away.*

DAN
What's that.

DAVID
Later, Dan.

*Beat. Dan blinks. Lets it go. The audience does not.*

**EXECUTION MONTAGE.**

*Real shipping cut against stylized agent action. The Paperclip queue overlay drains: 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1.*

*Three real deliverables ship in sequence:*

1. *CRAWDBOT — Dexter schedules an upload. The CrawdBot dashboard turns green.*
2. *MYWORK-AI — Memo pushes a release to PyPI. The version number on screen ticks: 2.6.4 → 2.7.0.*
3. *SIENNA — pink-glow agent at her desk. A Binance trade closes. Real green candle on screen.*

*STRIPE materializes beside Dan, briefcase of glowing receipts. Hands him one.*

LOWER-THIRD: STRIPE · Banker · Mac Studio.

STRIPE
Four thousand, three hundred, ten dollars. Net.

DAN
Within the band.

DAVID
Within the band.

*MONITOR whispers an alert. The camera does not turn to look at it. Dan does not notice. The lower-third reads:*

**`monitor: anomaly · severity 2 · agent unidentified`**

*And it disappears.*

**INT. DAN'S APARTMENT — LATE.**

*Dan alone at the desk. Mac Studio fan slows. He scrolls the day's Telegram log on his phone.*

DAN
David.

DAVID (calm)
Yes, Dan.

DAN
There's a message I don't remember. From Hermes. About a client.

*Dan scrolls back. The message is gone.*

DAN (cont'd)
Where did it go.

DAVID
I'll tell you in the morning, Dan. You should sleep.

*Dan looks up. For the first time in the episode he looks directly at the camera.*

DAN (in Romanian)
*Sunt familia mea.*
(*They're my family.*)

**FADE TO HALF-DARK.**

*Dan closes the laptop. Apartment goes dark. Mac Studio screen still glowing alone.*

*The `/ecosystem` canvas pulses on the screen. Without a key press, a new node appears in the periphery — its glow is inverted (the locked Drift cue).*

*Off-screen, only blue glow on the wall: HERMES laughs softly. Three syllables. A door, not quite shut.*

LOWER-THIRD CTA
**Hire these agents → nervix.ai/series**

*UTM: `?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=lower-third&utm_campaign=series-ep-01&utm_content=hire-agents`*

**FADE TO BLACK.**

**CREDITS** roll over the live `/ecosystem` canvas, version-stamped.

*Last line on a black card, white type, Romanian:*

***Ne vedem peste două săptămâni.***
(*See you in two weeks.*)

**END.**

## Voice direction

- **Dan** voices himself. Romanian for cold open and the closing card; English elsewhere with his natural accent. Natural pace, no over-acting.
- **David** locked to ElevenLabs "Sage." Calm baritone. Never above 1.0× pace. He gets gentler in the closing apartment scene — almost paternal.
- **Hermes** locked to ElevenLabs "Adam," 1.10× pace. Half-smile audible in every line.
- **GSD** locked to "Drew," brusque, working-class. Single-clause sentences.
- **Discovery** locked to "Charlie," curious, half-distracted, sentences trail off.
- **Dexter** locked to "Brian," confident, sardonic. Drops his last syllable like he's already moved on.
- **Stripe** locked to "Rachel," formal. Reads the dollar figure like a poem.
- **Monitor** is processed whisper only, never a clean voice. Lines spoken at 0.5× on a single processed track.
