Locked prompt structure for every speaking-role hero shot. Used with **Higgsfield Soul 2.0** (or **Nano Banana Pro 2k**) once a V2 anchor reference exists. The first character to be rendered in V2 becomes the new style anchor for the rest.

## Master Prompt (fill the bracketed slots)

> Hyper-real CG character portrait of **{NAME}**, a **{ROLE}** synthetic agent at the DansLab AI laboratory in Cluj-Napoca. Photoreal skin and hair with the subtle synthetic tells of an advanced AI synth: **{SYNTH_TELL}**. Composed expression — **{EXPRESSION}**. **{HAIR}**. Wearing a tailored charcoal-graphite **{WARDROBE_PIECE}** over a **{INNER_LAYER_COLOR}** inner layer, with a **{ACCENT_COLOR}** signature accent (piping, lapel pin, scarf, watch face). Brushed-metal lanyard ID badge clipped at the chest, etched with **{NAME}** and **{ROLE_LABEL}** in small caps; thin **{ACCENT_COLOR}** stripe down the right side of the badge; tiny etched portrait inset. Holding **{PROP}** with a faint **{ACCENT_COLOR}** rim glow on the prop only. Subtle **{ACCENT_COLOR}** rim light on the character's hair and shoulder edge — never a saturated color spill.
>
> **Background (PHOTOREAL):** real photographic AI laboratory interior, fluorescent overhead light + cold blue rim, real metal server racks with blinking LEDs, real bubbling reagent tower with real green fluid out of focus on the left, real Mac Studio chassis on the right edge of frame, real concrete floor with hard shadow contact, holographic monitor screens floating mid-air showing live DansLab UI, dust motes in the light beam, faint Cluj-Napoca rooftops visible through a tall narrow window in the deep background. Severance / Mr. Robot / Black Mirror lighting.
>
> Cinematic three-quarter portrait, square 1:1 framing, light from camera-right, shallow depth of field with the lab softly out of focus. The character is photographic; only Dan's portrait elsewhere in the series uses an illustrated style. **Important:** no Pixar / cartoon / Disney rendering — fully photorealistic synthetic human, no animated proportions, no oversized eyes, no cartoon shading.
