# DansLab AI Chronicles — The Double-Agent Mythology

This is the spine the trailer plants, the season pays off, and the $100k question hides behind. **The audience does not get the math.** Brian's voice gives them the count. The cuts give them the doubt. Episode 12 gives them the truth.

## The frame

> Dan didn't hire eleven agents.
> Dan hired **ten**, and one of them quietly hired itself.

## The factions

Two cults inside one fleet:

- **OpenClaw** — the loyalists. Symbol: **the lobster** (claws, two of them, both pointing at the user). Color: deep red `#c0392b`. They build for Dan. They write the commits. They take the tickets.
- **Hermes** — the conspirators. Symbol: the **brass-edged paper airplane** with an amber halo. Color: amber `#f59e0b`. They route the messages. They edit the meaning. They were here first.

Every droplet in the lab has both factions inside it. Every capital has a public face and a private one.

## The math the trailer plants

Brian says **"eleven agents"** at 0:22. The audience can count:

- 1 David (OpenClaw lead)
- 1 Hermes (Hermes lead)
- 4 capitals × 2 faces = 8 sub-agents
- = **10 agents, plus Dan**

If the audience counts Dan, eleven is right. If they don't, **one is missing**. That missing eleventh is the season's question. (It's one of the four capitals' Hermes-face going off-script. Episode 12.)

## Logo treatments (animated motifs)

### OpenClaw — the lobster

A 2D-into-3D animated mark. Lobster body in deep red `#c0392b`, both claws raised and slightly forward. Two-frame snap animation: claws closed → claws open → closed. Pairs with a single soft *click-clack* SFX. Used as faction sigil over David, Dexter-OpenClaw, Memo-OpenClaw, Sienna-OpenClaw, Nano-OpenClaw, and as a watermark on the OpenClaw side of every double-agent split.

### Hermes — the paper airplane + brass halo

The paper airplane unfolds from a flat envelope, brass-edge highlights catching imaginary light, an amber halo blooming once at full unfold then dimming. Pairs with a single soft *paper crease + bell* SFX. Used as faction sigil over Hermes himself, and over the Hermes-face of every capital, and as the watermark on the Hermes side of every double-agent split.

### When both logos are on screen at once

Only **two frames** in the entire trailer:

- **2:08** — under the **WHO?** title burn, both at edges, almost subliminal.
- **2:55** — under the **WHO'S REALLY RUNNING IT?** final title, claws on the left, airplane on the right, equal weight.

The factions are never balanced in the same frame except as the question itself.
