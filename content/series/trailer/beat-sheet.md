# DansLab AI Chronicles — Trailer Beat Sheet

**Length:** 3:00 (180s)
**Voice:** ElevenLabs · `Brian` · dry, confident, lightly conspiratorial
**Pace:** 70 BPM bed for first half → 110 BPM under stakes → drop to silence at the final question
**Visual lock:** V2 — Grounded Cinematic Realism. Real Cluj plates + real Mac Studio + holographic AR agent overlays. Mr. Robot / Severance grade.
**Loglines hammered three times:** "One man. Eleven AI agents. $100,000 by 2027."

## Audience-question beats (the spine)

These are the four direct-camera questions the trailer hangs on. Brian delivers each one cleanly, with one beat of silence after.

1. **0:08** — "What if I told you a man in Romania built a million-dollar AI company alone?"
2. **1:08** — "How did he do it?"
3. **1:30** — "Do you think it's possible?"
4. **2:55** — "Who's really running it?"

## Tonal arc

```
hook ──── setup ──── cast tease ──── stakes ──── question ──── conspiracy ──── climax ──── final
 cool      warm        cool            warm        cold         cold              hot         cold
```

The trailer cools the audience down twice on purpose — first when the cast is still mysterious, second when the question lands. Each cool beat sets up the heat that follows.

## What the trailer never shows

- David's full face (kept at 3/4 silhouette until E1 proper)
- Hermes' full reveal (he gets one frame at 1:55, half-amber half-shadow, eyes off-screen)
- The actual revenue numbers (we tick from $0 → ████████ → $100,000 with the middle blurred)
- Any agent's voice (only Brian and Dan speak — agents are mute in the trailer; they get voices in the show)

## Music & sound

- Bed: original score in the key of Mac Studio fan hum (low E). 70 BPM under acts 1–4, ramps to 110 BPM under acts 5–7, drops to silence under act 8.
- Sound design hits: Telegram bell (muted, stretched 3×), Mac Studio fan startup, single typewriter clack on each title burn, low UI pop on each on-screen question.
- Romanian line at 2:42 — Dan's own voice, no music, room tone only.
