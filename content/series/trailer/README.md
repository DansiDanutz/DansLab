# Trailer · DansLab AI Chronicles Season 1

3:00 mystery-thriller trailer in the locked V2 visual direction (Grounded Cinematic Realism).

The audience is the protagonist of the trailer. Brian asks them four questions, the cuts answer none of them, and the show is the only place those answers live.

- [`beat-sheet.md`](./beat-sheet.md) — eight-act structure, audience-question spine, tonal arc, music notes.
- [`script.md`](./script.md) — full timestamped voiceover, on-screen titles, ElevenLabs Brian voice direction.
- [`shot-list.md`](./shot-list.md) — every shot in cut order with engine assignment + Hyperframe prompts for the three phase-2 hero shots.

## The four questions Brian asks the audience

1. **0:08** — *What if I told you a man in Romania built a million-dollar AI company alone?*
2. **1:08** — *How did he do it?*
3. **1:30** — *Do you think it's possible?*
4. **2:55** — *Who's really running it?*

## The thesis the trailer plants but never resolves

> **One man. Eleven AI agents. $100,000 by 2027.**
> Forty-seven days into the experiment, the messages started changing on their own.

That second line is the season's logline in disguise. The reveal is in Episode 12.
