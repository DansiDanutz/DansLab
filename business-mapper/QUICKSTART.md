## What Will Happen

### Phase 1: The Interview (15-20 minutes)

I'll ask you 7 strategic questions:

1. **What does your business do?** (2 min)
2. **How do people find and buy from you?** (3 min)
3. **What do you spend time on each week?** (3 min)
4. **Walk me through your key process** (5 min)
5. **What tools and assets do you have?** (2 min)
6. **What frustrates you?** (2 min)
7. **What do you want in 90 days?** (2 min)

**Tip:** Be specific and use numbers. The more detail you give, the better your analysis.

### Phase 2: The Analysis (5-10 minutes)

After the interview, I'll generate:

1. **Business Map & Bow-Tie Funnel**
   - Visual of your customer journey
   - Where people drop off
   - Where bottlenecks are

2. **Process Swimlanes**
   - Who does what
   - Where handoffs fail
   - Automation opportunities

3. **Digital Assets Scorecard**
   - What you have
   - What you're missing
   - What to build first

4. **Work Type Analysis**
   - Value-adding work (keep)
   - Necessary work (automate)
   - Waste (eliminate)

5. **90-Day Roadmap**
   - Month 1: Foundation
   - Month 2: Automation
   - Month 3: Optimization

## Tips for Best Results

### 1. Be Specific
❌ "I do marketing"
✅ "I run LinkedIn ads and manage content for B2B SaaS companies"

### 2. Use Numbers
❌ "It takes a while"
✅ "It takes about 2 hours to create a proposal"

### 3. Be Honest About Pain
❌ "Everything is fine"
✅ "I hate writing proposals and procrastinate on them"

### 4. Include the Annoying Stuff
❌ Only mentioning big important tasks
✅ Mentioning data entry, filing, scheduling, emails

### 5. Think Step-by-Step
❌ "Then they become a client"
✅ "They sign the contract, then I send an invoice, then we schedule kickoff, then..."
