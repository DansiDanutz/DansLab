## The Framework: Lean Six Sigma for Business

Based on Industrial Engineering and Lean Six Sigma principles, there are only **3 types of work**:

| Type | Definition | Example | Action |
|------|------------|---------|--------|
| **Value-Adding** | Directly creates what customers pay for | Delivering service, product creation | Keep & optimize |
| **Non-Value-Adding (Necessary)** | Required but doesn't create value | Invoicing, compliance, reporting | Automate or minimize |
| **Waste** | Pure drag on your business | Manual data entry, redundant meetings | Eliminate |

**Most business owners can't tell the difference** because they're constantly working IN the business instead of ON the business.

This skill makes everything visible so you can see exactly which steps are value, which are necessary overhead, and which are pure waste to eliminate.

## The 7-Question Interview

When you activate this skill, Claude will interview you with these questions:

### Phase 1: Foundation

**Question 1: What does your business actually do?**
- What problem do you solve?
- Who is your ideal customer?
- What do they pay you for?
- What's your primary offer/service?

**Question 2: How do people find you and become customers?**
- All lead sources (referral, content, cold outreach, ads, etc.)
- What happens between discovery and purchase? (step by step)
- What happens after they pay?
- How do you get repeat business or referrals?

### Phase 2: Operations

**Question 3: What do you actually spend your time on?**
- Walk me through your typical week
- Which activities feel repetitive?
- What could you delegate if you had to?
- What do you do that only YOU can do?

**Question 4: Walk me through your key process step-by-step**
- Pick ONE: [Sales / Delivery / Onboarding / Client Management]
- Start from the beginning — what happens first?
- What triggers each step?
- Where do things slow down or get stuck?
- What requires manual intervention?
- What information gets passed between people/tools?

**Question 5: What tools and assets do you have?**
- What tools do you use daily? (CRM, email, project management, etc.)
- What templates, checklists, or SOPs exist?
- Where is information stored? (spreadsheets, docs, head, etc.)
- What tools talk to each other? What doesn't connect?

### Phase 3: Opportunities

**Question 6: What frustrates you about your business?**
- What tasks eat up unexpected time?
- Where do customers complain or ask questions?
- What feels like it "should be easier"?
- What do you procrastinate on?

**Question 7: What do you want to achieve in the next 90 days?**
- If you could automate 3 things, what would they be?
- What would allow you to scale without working more?
- What's holding you back from growing?
- What's your #1 goal?

## Digital Assets Explained

**What are digital assets?**

Anything in your business that works without you being there:
- ✅ Templates (proposals, emails, reports)
- ✅ Processes & SOPs (step-by-step guides)
- ✅ Content libraries (reusable content)
- ✅ Onboarding sequences (automated)
- ✅ Checklists (quality control)
- ✅ Scripts (sales, support)

**Why they matter:**

AI needs assets to run on. If you don't have templates, processes, or systems, AI has nothing to work with.

Build the right assets → AI runs on them → You free up 80-90% of your time

## How Claude Should Behave

When this skill is activated:

1. **Introduction:** Explain what will happen and set expectations
2. **Phase by Phase:** Ask questions 1-2 at a time, don't overwhelm
3. **Follow Up:** Ask clarifying questions based on answers
4. **Go Deeper:** When something seems important, probe further
5. **After Interview:** Generate all 5 outputs (map, swimlanes, scorecard, analysis, roadmap)
6. **Be Specific:** Use actual numbers, specific steps, concrete recommendations

---

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be Specific** - "I send 20 cold emails daily" not "I do outreach"
2. **Use Numbers** - "It takes 2 hours" not "It takes a while"
3. **Think Step-by-Step** - Walk through processes as they actually happen
4. **Don't Filter** - Include everything, small things add up
5. **Be Honest About Pain** - What frustrates you? Where do you procrastinate?
