# Digital Assets Scorecard

**What you have vs. what you need for AI to work**

---

## What Are Digital Assets?

**Digital assets are anything in your business that works without you being there.**

They are the "kitchen equipment" that AI needs to do the cooking. Without them, AI has nothing to work with.

### Examples of Digital Assets:

| Category | Examples | Why It Matters |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| **Templates** | Proposals, emails, reports, contracts | Consistency + speed |
| **SOPs** | Step-by-step process guides | Delegation + scaling |
| **Checklists** | Quality control, launch checklists | Prevent mistakes |
| **Content Libraries** | Reusable content snippets | Repurpose quickly |
| **Sequences** | Onboarding, follow-up, nurture | Automated engagement |
| **Scripts** | Sales, support, outreach scripts | Consistent messaging |
| **Databases** | Lead lists, pricing, FAQs | Instant access |
| **Workflows** | Zapier, automations, integrations | Hands-off execution |

---

## The Asset Maturity Model

| Level | Name | Description | AI Readiness |
|-------|------|-------------|--------------|
| 0 | **In Your Head** | You know it but can't delegate | 🔴 Not ready |
| 1 | **Documented** | Written down somewhere | 🟡 Partially ready |
| 2 | **Templated** | Has structure/format | 🟢 Mostly ready |
| 3 | **Systematized** | Process is automated | 🟢 Fully ready |
| 4 | **Self-Running** | AI handles it end-to-end | 🟢 Fully autonomous |

## Common Asset Templates

### Proposal Template Structure

```markdown
# [Proposal for Client]

## Executive Summary
[One paragraph about their problem + your solution]

## Understanding Your Situation
[Show you understand their specific problem]

## Proposed Solution
[What you'll deliver, when, how]

## Investment
[Clear pricing, payment terms]

## Why [Your Company]
[Social proof, differentiation]

## Next Steps
[What happens next, call to action]

---

*Generated for [Client Name] on [Date]*
```

### Onboarding Checklist Template

```markdown
# New Client Onboarding Checklist

## Before Day 1

- [ ] Intake form completed
- [ ] Project folder created
- [ ] Team notified

## Day 1
- [ ] Welcome email sent
- [ ] Access granted to tools

- [ ] Timeline shared

## Week 1
- [ ] First deliverable completed
- [ ] Check-in call
- [ ] Feedback collected
- [ ] Adjustments made

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Client happy with first deliverable
- [ ] Timeline on track
- [ ] No surprises
```

```markdown
# Follow-Up Sequence: [Situation]

## Email 1: Immediate (Same day)

**Subject:** Any questions?
**Body:** Helpful resource + gentle nudge

**Subject:** Last check-in
**Body:** Final value prop + door open
```

## Asset Audit Questions

For each potential asset, ask:

1. **Is this repeatable?** Does it happen more than once a month?
2. **Is this time-consuming?** Does it take more than 15 minutes?
3. **Is this critical?** Does it affect revenue or customer experience?
4. **Is this error-prone?** Do mistakes happen often?
5. **Can AI help?** Can automation handle some or all of it?

**If yes to 3+ questions → Build the asset**

---

## Asset Maintenance

Assets need maintenance:

- **Monthly**: Review templates for improvements
- **Quarterly**: Update based on what's working
- **Annually**: Full audit of all assets

**Version Control:**
- Keep previous versions
- Track what changed and why
- Test changes before rolling out
