The strategy, step by step

JEV supplies structured AI judgments inside a rule-based trading process. The scanner first ranks markets using a spread-and-liquidity heuristic. The entry model then supplies a BUY or SELL direction; routing filters can veto it before an order is considered.

Once a position exists, additions are a separate recovery decision. Price/setup evidence and the lifecycle's pinned rules determine whether an addition can be considered. JEV returns SUPPORT, WAIT or INVALID. Only SUPPORT together with every execution gate can proceed toward an order.

Profit-taking and trailing exits use net position profit and the thresholds saved for that lifecycle. A trailing rule tracks a profit peak and can request an exit after a configured giveback once armed. A submitted exit still needs venue acknowledgment and fill reconciliation.

How the system handles common problems

  • Weak or conflicting setups: filters can withhold entry and JEV can return WAIT or INVALID for additions.
  • Stale or malformed evidence: freshness and validation checks block new exposure rather than assume approval.
  • Duplicate orders or partial fills: pending-order and accounting checks reconcile confirmed fills before stage progression.
  • Profit giving back: pinned net-profit and trailing rules evaluate exits independently of a fresh AI approval.
  • Changing software: lifecycle policy pinning preserves the position's original rules. Reporting alone does not prove every live safeguard is currently working.

These mechanisms address failure modes; they do not establish a profitable edge. This page reports saved evidence and cannot start, stop or modify trading.

How to read this evidence

  • Published evidence and saved gate results describe observations, not readiness, restart authority, guaranteed profit, or protection from liquidation.
  • Entries and additions are separate decisions. A lifecycle allows at most one initial entry and two additions, and each addition must independently pass the current pinned policy's eligibility, freshness, capital, accounting, pending-order, and emergency gates.
  • A lifecycle advances stage only after completed fills. Its policy remains pinned for that lifecycle; net-profit and trailing thresholds come from that pinned policy rather than stale offline documentation.
  • The final stop is required only after the final stage is fully filled and the pinned policy requires it. A recorded stop does not guarantee execution price or prevent liquidation.
  • Model SUPPORT is one saved input, not an order, fill, or permission. Missing or invalid evidence fails closed for new exposure. Emergency handling is independent.
  • Manual or mixed-account activity can be excluded from reporting totals, but the raw account loss evidence remains visible in the retained audit.
